Open-source Clicky: Teachy is Clicky, but free and open source

Looking for Clicky? Teachy is the free, open-source continuation of Clicky, Farza's AI buddy that lives next to your cursor on the Mac. It is MIT-licensed, built in public on GitHub, and still shipping new releases.

What is Clicky?

Clicky is an open-source macOS app by Farza, released in April 2026 under the MIT licence at github.com/farzaa/clicky. It puts an AI buddy next to your cursor: it sees your screen, talks you through what you're doing, and points at things.

Clicky grew into HeyClicky, the official app for Mac and Windows at heyclicky.com.

Is Clicky still open source?

The original Clicky repository is still public and MIT-licensed, but it hasn't had a code change since April 2026. Its last update, in October 2026, was a README change pointing people to the official HeyClicky app. Development continues in HeyClicky.

Teachy forked Clicky's open code in July 2026 and has kept building in the open since: the app, the prompts and every fix are on GitHub to read, fork and change. If you want Clicky, but free, open source and maintained, that's Teachy.

What Teachy does

  • Answers about your screen, out loud. Hold control and option and ask; it starts speaking within about half a second.
  • Points the way. Its blue triangle flies to the button or menu you asked about.
  • Draws to explain. Diagrams, arrows and 3D scenes over your screen, narrated as they appear.
  • Does the job. Hand off a task and keep working; it asks before it clicks, types or runs anything.
  • Types what you say. Hold fn and control in any text field and talk.
  • Remembers you. Its memory is two Markdown files on your Mac that you can open and edit.

Install Teachy

  1. Download Teachy.dmg and drag Teachy to Applications.
  2. Right-click Teachy and choose Open, then Open again. The free build isn't notarised by Apple yet, so macOS asks once.
  3. Grant the permissions it walks you through, then sign in, or choose Use my own key and paste an OpenRouter key.
  4. Hold control and option and ask about your screen.

Questions about open-source Clicky

Is there an open-source version of Clicky?

Yes. Clicky's original code is open source under the MIT licence at github.com/farzaa/clicky, but it hasn't had a code change since April 2026. Teachy is built on that code and is the open-source version that is still being developed: free, MIT-licensed, with new releases.

Is Teachy free?

Yes. Teachy is free to download and use, and its code is free to read, change and share under the MIT licence.

Is Teachy made by HeyClicky or by Farza?

No. Teachy is an independent project built on Farza's open-source Clicky code, credited in its licence. It is not affiliated with HeyClicky.

What can Teachy do?

Hold control and option and ask about anything on your screen. Teachy answers out loud, points at the button you need, draws diagrams over your screen, types what you dictate, remembers you in a file you can edit, and can run tasks in the background, asking before it clicks, types or runs anything.

Does Teachy work on Windows?

The download is for Macs with Apple silicon on macOS 14.2 or later. A Windows port is in the repository but isn't released yet.

Does Teachy watch my screen all the time?

No. It only sees your screen while you hold the shortcut, or when you turn on hands-free voice.

Security

Security and data control

Accelerating AI adoption without governance creates exposure. Teachy is designed so fluency and control arrive together.

Screen context is read only during a coaching moment and routes through your proxy. Nothing is stored or sold. Restricted work stays off public models.

Policy enforcement

You define what is sensitive and where it may go. Teachy blocks incorrect placement in the moment and redirects people to the approved system.

Stack alignment

Configured to your internal software, operating procedures, and licensed AI tools — so coaching matches how your company actually works.

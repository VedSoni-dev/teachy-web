how it works

you act. teachy reacts. no video to pause.

  1. 1

    ask out loud

    hold ctrl + option and talk. teachy answers in speech and walks you through the real app — not a tutorial tab.

  2. 2

    it points & waits

    the blue cursor points at the next move, waits for you to click or type, then coaches the next step — the reaction loop you see in the window above.

  3. 3

    checks & remembers

    it verifies the outcome before moving on, and keeps a model of what you already know — so it stops re-explaining git and reviews what you’re about to forget.

frequently asked questions

what to know about teachy, privacy, and getting started.

what is teachy?

a free menu-bar app for mac. it watches your screen, sits next to your cursor, and coaches you 1:1 through AI tools, software, code, and real work on your own machine.

is it free?

yes. teachy for individuals is free and doesn't require an account to start. teachy for teams is a separate, bespoke offering for companies.

is my screen being watched all the time?

no. teachy only looks at your screen during an active coaching session or push-to-talk moment. all API calls route through the teachy proxy; no keys or screen data are baked into the app itself.

what can teachy actually teach me?

anything you can do on your mac. AI tools like claude and chatgpt, software like VS Code and figma, how to build a website, write code, ship a workflow, and more.

which mac do i need?

macOS 14.2 or later, for the screen-capture APIs teachy relies on to see what you're doing.

what's the difference between teachy and teachy enterprise?

teachy is free and built for individuals. teachy enterprise adds bespoke AI systems plus custom curriculum built around your company's tools, SOPs, and roles, priced per engagement.

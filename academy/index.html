How it works

Four steps. That’s the whole loop.

Same engine for individuals and enterprises — teach anything by doing.

  1. 1

    Teachy points

    It shows exactly where to look and what to do — on the real screen, beside the cursor.

  2. 2

    You do it

    The learner acts first. Practice on ChatGPT, code, GitHub, your internal tools — not a demo video.

  3. 3

    Teachy checks

    It looks at the screen, confirms the outcome, and only then advances. Real skills, verified.

  4. 4

    It remembers

    Progress, confusions, and wins stay with the learner — so coaching gets more personal over time.

Questions

Frequently asked questions

What to know about Teachy, privacy, and getting started.

What is Teachy?

A free menu-bar app for Mac. It watches your screen, sits next to your cursor, and coaches you 1:1 through real AI tools and real work — websites, agents, Claude, ChatGPT, and more.

Is it free?

Yes. Teachy for individuals is free, open source, and doesn’t require an account to start. Teachy Enterprise is a separate, bespoke offering for companies.

Is my screen being watched all the time?

No — Teachy only looks at your screen during an active coaching session or push-to-talk moment. All API calls route through the Teachy proxy; no keys or screen data are baked into the app itself.

What can Teachy actually teach me?

How to use tools you already have open — Claude, ChatGPT, agents, Wispr Flow — and how to do real work with them, like building and deploying a website, on your own Mac.

Which Mac do I need?

macOS 14.2 or later, for the screen-capture APIs Teachy relies on to see what you're doing.

What’s the difference between Teachy and Teachy Enterprise?

Teachy is free and built for individuals. Teachy Enterprise adds bespoke AI systems plus custom curriculum built around your company’s tools, SOPs, and roles — priced per engagement.

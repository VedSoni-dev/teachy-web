Is Teachy made by HeyClicky?

No. Teachy grew from Clicky’s open-source code and is built separately, in the open. It isn’t affiliated with HeyClicky.

Is Teachy free?

Yes. The app is free to download and the code is free to read and change.

Does Teachy watch my screen all the time?

No. It only sees your screen while you hold the shortcut, or when you turn on hands-free.

Can Teachy change things without asking?

No. Reading is free, but clicking, typing and commands each ask you first. Press Escape or move the mouse to stop a job.

Which Mac does Teachy need?

Any Mac on macOS 14.2 Sonoma or later.

Why does macOS warn me about Teachy?

The free build is not notarised by Apple yet. Right-click the app, choose Open, and macOS remembers your choice.

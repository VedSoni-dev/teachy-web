// why teachy exists

The original went private.
We stayed open.

Clicky showed what an AI next to your cursor feels like. Its first codebase stays open, but the new work went private in April. Teachy picks up the open line and keeps building in public: every feature, fix and late-night commit lands on GitHub where you can read it, fork it, and make it yours.

  • 01
    Read every line. The app, the proxy, the prompts. No black box watching your screen.
  • 02
    Maintained, not abandoned. Dated releases, a real changelog, and commits you can watch land.
  • 03
    Your key, your bill. Bring your own AI key. Nothing to subscribe to.

How it works

Four steps. That’s the whole loop.

No videos to watch. You learn by doing — Teachy coaches right beside you.

  1. 1

    Teachy points

    It shows you exactly where to look and what to do — on your screen, beside your cursor.

  2. 2

    You do it

    You act first. Teachy waits while you practice instead of making you watch someone else do it.

  3. 3

    Teachy checks

    It looks at your screen, confirms you actually did it, and nudges you to the next step.

  4. 4

    It remembers

    Your work stays on your Mac, so help stays specific to what you’re building that day.

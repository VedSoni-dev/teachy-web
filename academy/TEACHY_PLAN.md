# Teachy — World Platform & Business Plan

**North star:** Become the default way the world learns to do real work on a computer — free, 1:1, act-first.

**Business:** Two products, one engine.

| Product | Who | Offer |
|---------|-----|--------|
| **Teachy** | Individuals | Free tutor that can teach anything on the real Mac |
| **Teachy Enterprise** | Companies | Bespoke AI + Teachy for employee **AI fluency** |

**Wedge:** Practice on the real screen (point → act → check), not another video player. AI fluency is the new literacy.
